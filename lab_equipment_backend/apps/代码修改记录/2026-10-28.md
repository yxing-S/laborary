## 一、删除代码记录

| 文件 | 删除范围 | 说明 |
|------|---------|------|
| apps/equipment/views.py | `equipment_list` 手写分页（page/size 参数、`{total, pages, current, records}` 结构） | 改用公共 `paginate()`，参数 page/pageSize，返回 `{list, total, page, pageSize, totalPages}` |
| apps/equipment/views.py | `Result.success(msg="设备已删除")` | 必崩 bug：Result.success 关键字是 message，传 msg= 会 TypeError |
| apps/equipment/serializers.py | `EquipmentOutSerializer`（`equipmentId` 别名）、`EquipmentDetailSerializer`（`historyRecords` 字段、多余 `userId/returnType/createTime`） | 字段名对齐文档：主键统一 `id`，历史记录统一 `borrowHistory`（id/userName/userNo/planBorrowStart/planReturnTime/actualReturnTime/approvalStatus/overdueStatus） |
| apps/equipment/urls.py | 旧路径 `equipment/list`、`admin/equipment/add`、`admin/equipment/update/{id}`、`admin/equipment/delete/{id}`、`admin/export/equipment` | 对齐文档：`GET /equipment`、`POST /admin/equipment`、`PUT /admin/equipment/{id}`、`DELETE /admin/equipment/{id}`、`GET /admin/equipment/export` |
| apps/user/urls.py | `users/me`、`admin/users`、`admin/users/{id}` 同路径重复注册两条 path | Django resolver 只命中第一个 pattern，第二条永不命中（POST/PUT/DELETE 被 405）；合并为单条 + method_dispatch |
